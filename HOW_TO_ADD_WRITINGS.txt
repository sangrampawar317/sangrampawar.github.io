# How to Add and Edit Your Own Poetry & Articles

You have complete control over your writings. There are **zero** AI-generated or placeholder articles.

### 📝 To Add Your Poetry or Article:
1. Open the folder `sangram-pawar-personal-portfolio/`.
2. Open the file `js/main.js` with Notepad, TextEdit, or VS Code.
3. Look for line 48:
   ```javascript
   const myWritings = [
   ```
4. Paste your poem or article inside the brackets `[` and `]`, like this:

```javascript
  const myWritings = [
    {
      id: 1,
      title: "तुमच्या कवितेचे किंवा लेखाचे नाव (Title)",
      category: "poetry", // Type 'poetry' for poems, or 'article' for essays
      categoryLabel: "Poetry (कविता)",
      date: "2026",
      readTime: "2 min read",
      excerpt: "कवितेची किंवा लेखाची एक ओळ सारांश (Brief 1-line summary)",
      content: `
        <div class="poetry-box">
येथे तुमची कविता किंवा लेख लिहा.
प्रत्येक ओळ नवीन ओळीवर टाका.
जसे तुम्हाला हवे तसे स्वरूप द्या.
        </div>
      `
    }
  ];
```

5. Save the file (`Ctrl + S` or `Cmd + S`).
6. Refresh `index.html` in your browser. Your poem or article will automatically appear with its full interactive popup reader and search filter!
